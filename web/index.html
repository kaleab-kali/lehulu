Sign-up that works for everyone

Lehulu lets a learner join ALX Savanna and apply for jobs by typing or by voice, in Amharic or English, with no ID document needed. It never asks about disability. It asks how you use your phone, and adapts to that.

  • 2languages: Amharic and English
  • 0time limits, and nothing is lost offline
  • 1line of code to add it to any site

Try each part

  • Learner Sign up one question at a time. Choose “I listen”, “Pictures” or “Big and simple” and watch the screens change.
  • Partner, such as AAAPD Confirm a learner who has no ID document, or register someone who is with you.
  • Employer Post a job with the support it offers, and read applications, by text or by voice.
  • Jobs and my profile Jobs sorted by the support you need. Sign in on another phone, change answers, download or delete your data.
  • Inside Savanna The same sign-up inside the real Savanna pages, in Savanna’s colours. Savanna adapts when the learner agrees.

How to test it

Start at Sign up. The second question, “How do you use your phone?”, changes every screen after it. Pick what matches the person testing, or try each one in turn.

Every answer is kept as it is given. Close the page half-way, open it again, and the answers are still there.

About this test system

  • Sign-in codes are shown on screen. On a real system they arrive by SMS. Everything else works the same way.
  • Partner and employer screens offer one-tap test accounts. On a real system each organisation gets its own login.
  • Not included yet: recorded Amharic question audio and Ethiopian Sign Language videos (they must be recorded by people), and a connection to a real SMS company. Until the audio is recorded, “Play question” uses the phone’s own voice where it has one for the language, and is hidden otherwise.
  • Everything here is test data. Use any name and a number like 0911 234 567.
