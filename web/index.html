Sign-up that works for everyone

Lehulu is the sign-up, profile and job-application part of a learning platform. A learner joins by typing or by voice, in Amharic or English, without an ID document. Lehulu never asks about disability. It asks how the learner uses their phone, and every screen follows that answer.

Sign up Add it to your platform

How it works

  1. The learner says how they use their phone. I listen with a screen reader, I speak my answers, pictures and audio, big and simple, sign language, or save my data. Nothing about diagnosis.
  2. Every screen adapts. One question at a time, read aloud or answered by voice, picture choices, large buttons. There is no time limit, and each answer is saved as it is given.
  3. No ID document is needed. A partner organisation such as AAAPD confirms who the learner is, or registers someone who is with them. The learner agrees with a code on their own phone.
  4. The learner applies for jobs. Jobs offering the support they need come first, and they decide, job by job, whether the employer sees their support needs.
  5. The platform adapts too, if the learner agrees. Savanna can enlarge its text, give extra time on quizzes, or show its lessons with sign language, from the same answer.

Who it works for

  • Blind learnersEvery question and every error is announced by the screen reader, and the cursor goes where it is needed.
  • Learners with low visionLarge text and buttons, strong contrast, and nothing cut off at 400% zoom.
  • Deaf learnersEverything is written; nothing needs sound. Sign language videos for each question, and interpreters for interviews.
  • Learners who cannot type easilyA spoken answer counts as the answer. Every control works by keyboard or switch, with no dragging.
  • Learners who read littlePicture choices, questions read aloud, and the name given by voice.
  • Learners on a slow or costly connectionAbout 110 KB, videos only when tapped, and nothing lost if the signal drops.

Four screens

  • Sign up For the learner, in Amharic or English.
  • Jobs and my profile Apply for jobs; change, download or delete your data.
  • Partner Confirm a learner with no ID, or register one.
  • Employer Post a job with the support it offers; read applications.
  • Inside Savanna The same sign-up inside the Savanna dashboard.

Add it to your platform

1. Load the script once, and place the sign-up where it should appear.

<script type="module" src="https://lehulu.com/lehulu.js"></script>
<lehulu-signup lang="am" jobs-url="/jobs" profile-url="/profile"></lehulu-signup>

2. Listen for what the learner did. Your platform decides what happens next.

document.addEventListener('lehulu:signup-complete', (e) => {
  // e.detail: { learnerId, idStatus, linkToken }. Exchange linkToken on your server at POST /v1/link.
});
document.addEventListener('lehulu:preferences', (e) => {
  // Sent only if the learner agreed. e.detail: { language, accessPreferences }
});

3. The other screens work the same way: <lehulu-profile>, <lehulu-jobs>, <lehulu-partner>, <lehulu-employer>. Sites that block scripts can use an iframe (/embed/?flow=signup&lang=am), and servers can use the REST API. The plain page example shows the sign-up with no other styles around it.
